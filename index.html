CleanBeats Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

CleanBeats is an iOS app for browsing and playing Apple Music content while filtering explicit content and blocking selected songs, albums, and artists. This policy explains what information CleanBeats uses, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Information CleanBeats uses

CleanBeats does not require an account. When you authorize access, CleanBeats uses Apple Music and MusicKit to access the library and catalog information needed to display, search, filter, and play music. Catalog search terms and the requests needed to retrieve music information or start playback are sent to Apple through MusicKit. CleanBeats does not receive or store your Apple ID password.

CleanBeats stores app settings on your device, including explicit content preferences, blocked songs, albums, and artists, and the identifiers needed to match library items with catalog items. Recent search terms are also stored on your device so they can be shown in Search. These records are not sent to a CleanBeats-operated server.

The app writes some request errors to device or development diagnostic logs. An error message can include a music item identifier. CleanBeats does not operate an analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service and does not send these app diagnostic messages to a CleanBeats server. Apple may receive system diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's policies.

How information is used and shared

On-device settings and identifiers are used to apply your content preferences, remember blocked content, and match music items. Music library, catalog search, and playback requests are processed by Apple services through MusicKit. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and the terms for Apple Music and MusicKit.

CleanBeats has no account system or CleanBeats-operated backend, and does not sell personal information or use advertising or analytics partners. If you choose to email support, the developer receives the email address and message you send and uses them to respond to your request. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve the request and maintain necessary records. You can ask for deletion by emailing the address below; legal obligations may require some records to be retained.

This policy is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Retention and deletion

CleanBeats keeps its settings, blocked-content records, item mappings, and recent searches in app storage on your device. Recent searches can be removed with the Clear Recent Searches control. You can unblock content and change preferences in the app. Removing CleanBeats from your device removes its local app data; copies may remain in device backups according to your Apple backup settings.

CleanBeats does not maintain a server-side account or music-library database to delete. To stop future library access, revoke CleanBeats' Media & Apple Music permission in iOS Settings.
